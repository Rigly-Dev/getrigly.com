Rigly is in public beta. It's ready for everyday use, and we use it ourselves, but you may find rough edges, and things will change as we hear from you.

## What beta means

- **Updates come often.** Rigly checks daily and updates itself, so fixes reach you quickly.
- **Your projects are safe.** Rigly never changes your project files, except when you ask it to: creating a site, or a workspace's copy.
- **Back up databases you care about.** Rigly's databases are for local development. Keep a copy of anything you'd miss, as you would with any development tool.
- **Some things may change**, such as settings, commands and defaults, before version 1.0.

## Send feedback

Bug reports, ideas and questions all go to [Rigly's issue tracker on GitHub](https://github.com/Rigly-Dev/rigly-releases/issues). You need a free GitHub account to post.

The quickest way is from Rigly itself: choose **Send Feedback (Beta)** in Rigly's menu bar menu, or click the **Beta** badge in Rigly's window. Rigly opens the form with your Rigly and macOS versions already filled in.

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/Rigly-Dev/rigly-releases/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
- [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/Rigly-Dev/rigly-releases/issues/new?template=feature.yml)
- [Ask a question](https://github.com/Rigly-Dev/rigly-releases/issues/new?template=question.yml)

Before reporting a bug, have a look at the [open issues](https://github.com/Rigly-Dev/rigly-releases/issues): someone may have reported it already. Add a 👍 to it so we know it affects you too.

## A good bug report

- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- The output of `rigly doctor`, which shows Rigly's checks.
- Anything relevant from the **Logs** window. Remove anything private first.
- Other development tools on your Mac, such as Herd, Valet, MAMP or Docker, and whether you use a VPN.

The [troubleshooting guide](/docs/troubleshooting/) solves the most common problems, if you'd like to try first.
